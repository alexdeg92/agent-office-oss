## Diagnosis from our own screens

The problem is a weak visual hierarchy repeated across contexts: unrelated values receive equal boxes; controls inherit grid width; a caption appears under every input; labels and metadata outweigh agent identity; counters and global settings displace the mobile scene. The [Settings baseline](../reports/designer-01.md) and [owner correction](../reports/designer-09.md) supply screenshot evidence. Fix the hierarchy and control anatomy in every direction, then compare genuine differences in personality.
